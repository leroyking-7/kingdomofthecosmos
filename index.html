Architecting the Infinite. Exploring the Elaren System.

The Architect: Leroy King

"I don’t just write stories; I build systems."
With a background in networking and hardware, I look at the stars and see a framework. For me, creating the Kingdom of the Cosmos—the universe where Nuai Nani Rising takes place—is a matter of understanding what is possible.
My technical expertise allows me to construct the Elaren system with a foundation of logic: knowing exactly how things connect, what technology can achieve, and where the absolute limits of the physical world lie.

But logic is only half the journey.
While my professional life is governed by hardware and code, my stories are born in the unstructured depths of the mind. I dream in galaxies. I wake up with the sights and sounds of worlds that don't exist yet, bringing them to life through a process that is as much about spiritual connection as it is about political intrigue.
Writing is my way of merging the rigid laws of engineering with the boundless possibilities of the subconscious.

Welcome to the Elaren system. Let’s see how far the connection goes."
