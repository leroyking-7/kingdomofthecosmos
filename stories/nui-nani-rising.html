Nui Nani Rising

The truth was burnt. From ashes, it rises.

Location: Veridora, in the Elaren system

Era:Tidal Cycle VI – The Age of Dominion

Prologue

The surface of Nui Nani shimmered under the twin suns. Endless oceans stretched far beyond the horizon, broken only by the humming coral towers of the Anari clans. Beneath the waves, something ancient stirred — a myth reborn, and a voice long silenced beginning to whisper again.

Kaela, daughter of the tide-priest, dove deep into the blue. The light faded, but her path was lit by intuition, by dreams she couldn't explain. She was being called.

Chapter I: Echoes in the Deep

“You will find it in the breath between silence,” her grandmother once said. Kaela never understood until now, suspended in the deep, as glowing glyphs awakened on an ancient ruin — a relic thought to be legend.

Something stirred within her — not fear, but recognition.
